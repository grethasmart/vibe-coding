Shrimp & Broccoli

Prep: 15 min | Cook: 20 min | Serves: 2-3
View Recipe Notes →

Ingredients

  • Shrimp
  • Broccoli
  • 1 tbsp Vegetable Oil
  • 1 tbsp Sesame Oil
  • Chopped/Shredded Garlic
  • Oyster Sauce
  • Chili Oil (optional)
  • Scallions (optional)
  • White Rice
  • seasonings(salt, pepper, ginger optional)

Preparation

Clean and devein the shrimp. Trim the broccoli ends and cut the crowns into bite-sized pieces.

Instructions

  1. Heat vegetable and sesame oil over medium-high heat. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant.
  2. Add in brocolli and sauté until three-quarters done.
  3. Add the seasoned shrimp and continue to sauté until cooked through.
  4. Stir in oyster sauce and optional chili oil; let it bubble to coat.
  5. Remove from heat and serve over white rice, garnished with scallions.
