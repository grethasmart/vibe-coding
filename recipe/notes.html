Recipe Notes

Shrimp & Broccoli

Chef's Tips

  • Chili oils I like: Momofuku Original Chili Crunch, and Fly By Jing Sichuan Chili Crisp (note: this one is extra spicy!).
  • Fresh ginger adds a little extra flavor. If you don't have fresh ginger, you can use ginger powder to give it some extra umph.
  • Sesame seeds are also nice to use as a finishing garnish.
